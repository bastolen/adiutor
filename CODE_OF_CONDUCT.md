# Code of Conduct

Adiutor follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
By taking part in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions or any other space) you agree
to follow it.

## In short

- Be welcoming, kind and respectful, including towards people whose views and experience differ
  from yours.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.
- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks and publishing other people's
  private information are not tolerated.

The full text linked above is what applies; this summary is only a reminder.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email **adiutor@bastolen.nl**. Reports are
handled confidentially, and the maintainers will respond as quickly as they can. Maintainers who
don't follow the Code of Conduct may face the same consequences as anyone else.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the impact.
